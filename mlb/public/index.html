New to baseball?

A home run is a ball hit over the outfield wall. Four numbers explain almost every one.

Exit velocityHow fast the ball leaves the bat. 95 mph is hit hard. Over 110 is rare.
Launch angleHow steeply it rises. Most homers leave at 25° to 35°. Under 10° is a grounder.
ParksIn how many of the 30 big-league ballparks the same hit would have been a home run.
BarrelThe ideal mix of speed and angle. Barrels usually go for extra bases or leave the park.
