What qualifies for this screen?

Full-game, two-way prices are compared within the same provider event, phase, market and line. Pinnacle or Circa provide a power-method no-vig benchmark; when both qualify, the lower probability is used.

Integer spreads, totals and NFL moneylines are excluded because a push model is required. Incomplete, suspended, conflicting, expired and unusually large (>15%) discrepancies are excluded. This screen covers sportsbooks. Exchange and prediction-market pricing requires separate fee and liquidity validation. Live prices require a healthy stream. Delivery-only timestamps are identified on the card.

Book availability and settlement rules must be checked with the sportsbook. A selection link is opened only if the provider supports that region. Clicking never records a placed wager.
